# 03_ENVIRONMENTAL_ROBUSTNESS — High-Altitude Environmental Effects

> Active research area for the Planning & Deep Research phase.

## What Belongs Here

- Effects of **extreme cold** on electronics, batteries (Li-ion/LiFePO4), LCD/OLED, servos, lubricants
- Effects of **low pressure** on thermal dissipation, capacitors, sealed enclosures
- Effects of **dust & snow** on optics, connectors, ventilation, RF performance
- Effects of **high wind** on gimbal stability, pointing accuracy, structural loading
- **Micro-radian pointing/tracking accuracy** retention — what degrades it, how to compensate
- Hardening techniques: IP65 enclosures, conformal coating, heated compartments, TCXO oscillators, silicone/PTFE cables
- Thermal management & battery chemistry at low temperature
- Cold-box test protocol design and quantified degradation curves

## Solution Approach

AI-powered underground mine safety system with gas detection, personnel tracking, and emergency rescue communication. ESP32-C3 nodes monitor gas levels (CH4, CO, O2) and send alerts via MQTT; Pi 4B runs edge processing and safety dashboard; ESP32-WROOM provides WiFi AP for rescue team communication.

## Solution Approach

Smart water purification and quality monitoring system for rural and mining-affected areas. ESP32-C3 reads water quality sensors (pH, turbidity, TDS) via GPIO/ADC; Pi 4B runs purification control logic and quality dashboard; OLED displays real-time water quality metrics; MQTT sends contamination alerts.
